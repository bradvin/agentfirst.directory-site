## Variant: Search-first editorial

### Design stance
Get search and real listings into the first viewport while keeping a short explanation of the evidence standard.

### Key choices
- Layout: left-aligned editorial hero, compact evidence sidebar, search immediately below, two-column result grid.
- Typography: keeps IBM Plex Mono for identity and headings; IBM Plex Sans for longer copy.
- Color: reuses the current near-black, grey, and teal system.
- Interaction: live search, category filtering, and expandable category row.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: immediate discovery, readable hierarchy, wider desktop usage, fewer repeated controls.
- Weak at: less room for educational copy and category browsing than the guided option.

### Best for
- Visitors who already know roughly what they need and want to search or scan quickly.
